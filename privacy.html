Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

TW Stock Heat Monitor ("the App") is a personal, non-commercial research tool that measures how often Taiwan-listed stocks are mentioned in public Threads posts.

Data we access

  • Public Threads posts returned by the Threads API keyword search (post text, timestamp, permalink, post ID).
  • The access token of the App owner's own Threads account, used only to call the API.

How we use data

Data is used only to compute aggregated mention counts per stock per day for personal research. We do not sell, share, or publish individual posts or user information, and we do not build profiles of individual users.

Storage and retention

Data is stored locally on the owner's computer. Raw post text is kept only as long as needed to compute aggregate statistics.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you, email r19991204@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.
